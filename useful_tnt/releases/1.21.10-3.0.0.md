- Mod renamed from Cheaper TNT to Useful TNT — remove the old JAR and install this one
- Config moved to `config/useful_tnt.json` (auto-migrates from `cheaper_tnt.json`)
- TNT explosions no longer destroy item entities, so quarry and chain blasts keep their loot
- Cheaper recipe toggle is unchanged
- Right-click the air with TNT in your main hand and flint and steel or a fire charge in your off hand to drop a lit TNT at your feet
- That TNT uses a 5-second fuse so you can walk away; holding right-click does not spawn extra bombs
- The dropped TNT keeps your motion (or the vehicle you are riding), including up/down speed
- Looking at a block still places unlit TNT as usual
